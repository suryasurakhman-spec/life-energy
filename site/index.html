An hour is worth less than your payslip says.

Take away what the job costs you — the commute, the lunches, the clothes — and count every hour it really takes. What remains is your real wage. Every price you see is measured against it.

Point. Read. Decide.

  1. I

    Point

    The camera is the home screen. Hold it over a shelf tag, a menu or a price on a screen. No photo is taken.

  2. II

    Read

    Each price gains a small chip in hours of your life. The chip deepens as the cost grows.

    • 12 munder 15 min
    • 40 munder 1 hour
    • 4 h 46 mwithin a work day
    • 2.5 work daysup to a week
    • 6.5 work weeksbeyond a week
  3. III

    Decide

    Tap a chip and answer one question: worth it to you? Log it, or set it back on the shelf. No verdict either way.
