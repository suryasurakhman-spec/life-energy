Privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026

The short version

  • Your wage, prices, spending and logs stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Camera images are read in memory and thrown away. They are never saved or uploaded.
  • There is no account and no sign-up. We hold no personal data about you.
  • No ads, no trackers, no selling of data.
  • Profile → Delete all data deletes everything on the phone, right away.

Who we are

Life Energy is made by [Operator name] (“we”, “us”), [postal address]. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: [contact email].

What stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device, in its own private storage:

  • Your wage details: pay, pay period, hours, and job costs and job hours, plus the real hourly wage worked out from them.
  • Prices you scan or type, the expenses you log, and your “worth it?” answers.
  • Your settings, such as language and home currency.
  • A copy of the daily exchange rates.

We have no copy of any of it. All calculations happen on the phone.

Camera. When the price lens is open, camera frames are read in memory to find prices and then discarded. A frozen frame is discarded when you close it. No image is written to storage or sent anywhere. The app asks for camera permission only to do this.

Phone backups. If you turn on Android backup or iCloud backup, your phone may include the app’s data in that backup, held by Google or Apple under your own account. We cannot read these backups.

What leaves your phone, and why

The app has no account and no server of ours that receives your data. Only the following connections are made:

Exchange rates

To convert prices in other currencies, the app downloads the European Central Bank’s public daily exchange-rate file when its copy is more than 12 hours old. Nothing about you is sent with the request. Like any website, the ECB’s server sees your IP address.

Text recognition

Prices are read by Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone. Images and recognised text are never sent to Google or to us. On Android, Google Play services may download recognition models. ML Kit may also send Google basic diagnostic information, such as device model, system version, app version and performance figures, as described in Google’s ML Kit terms.

App stores

Apple and Google handle downloads and app updates. If you choose to share crash reports or diagnostics with developers in your phone’s settings, they may pass us crash reports without your name.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data, or share it for advertising.
  • The app has no usage analytics. If that changes, we will update this policy first. Analytics would never include amounts or prices, and you could turn it off.

How long it is kept

Data on your phone stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. We keep nothing, because we receive nothing.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Profile → Export my data makes a CSV or JSON file of everything the app holds, which you can save or share.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete all data deletes everything the app holds on the phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; your data is in your hands on the phone. Questions: email [contact email] and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

The app’s data lives in its private storage on the phone, which other apps cannot read. The exchange-rate download uses an encrypted connection. Lock your phone to keep the data private.

Children

Life Energy is not meant for children under 13. The app collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If a change affects what leaves your phone, such as optional sign-in and sync in a later version, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
