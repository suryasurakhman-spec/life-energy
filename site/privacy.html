Privacy policy

Effective 13 October 2026

The short version

  • Your wage, prices, spending and logs stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Camera images are read in memory and thrown away. They are never saved or uploaded.
  • To sign in, we keep your email address or your Apple or Google sign-in ID. That is the only thing we store about you.
  • No ads, no trackers, no selling of data.
  • Profile → Delete all data deletes your account and everything on the phone, right away.

Who we are

Life Energy is made by [Operator name] (“we”, “us”), [postal address]. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: [contact email].

What stays on your phone

The app stores the following only on your device, in its own private storage:

  • Your wage details: pay, pay period, hours, and job costs and job hours, plus the real hourly wage worked out from them.
  • Prices you scan or type, the expenses you log, and your “worth it?” answers.
  • Your settings, such as language and home currency.
  • A copy of the daily exchange rates.

We have no copy of any of it. All calculations happen on the phone.

Camera. When the price lens is open, camera frames are read in memory to find prices and then discarded. A frozen frame is discarded when you close it. No image is written to storage or sent anywhere. The app asks for camera permission only to do this.

Phone backups. If you turn on Android backup or iCloud backup, your phone may include the app’s data in that backup, held by Google or Apple under your own account. We cannot read these backups.

What leaves your phone, and why

Your sign-in account

You sign in once before first use. The account is handled by our provider Supabase, on servers in [Supabase region]. We store:

  • Email link: your email address. We send a one-time sign-in link to it through [email provider].
  • Sign in with Apple: your Apple user ID and the email address Apple shares, which can be a private relay address if you chose “Hide My Email”.
  • Sign in with Google: your Google account ID, email address, and the name and profile picture link Google shares.
  • When the account was created and when you last signed in.

Apple or Google confirm who you are. They know you signed in to Life Energy, under their own privacy policies.

To keep sign-in secure, Supabase’s servers log each sign-in request, including your IP address and the app version. These logs are kept for a short time, days rather than months, and are used only to run and protect the service.

The account is not used for anything else. No prices, wages or spending are attached to it, and once you are signed in the app works without a connection.

Exchange rates

To convert prices in other currencies, the app downloads the European Central Bank’s public daily exchange-rate file when its copy is more than 12 hours old. Nothing about you is sent with the request. Like any website, the ECB’s server sees your IP address.

Text recognition

Prices are read by Google ML Kit, which runs on the phone. Images and recognised text are never sent to Google or to us. On Android, Google Play services may download recognition models. ML Kit may also send Google basic diagnostic information, such as device model, system version, app version and performance figures, as described in Google’s ML Kit terms.

App stores

Apple and Google handle downloads and app updates. If you choose to share crash reports or diagnostics with developers in your phone’s settings, they may pass us crash reports without your name.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data, or share it for advertising.
  • The app has no usage analytics. If that changes, we will update this policy first. Analytics would never include amounts or prices, and you could turn it off.

Legal basis

Where data-protection law such as the GDPR applies, we keep your sign-in account because it is needed to provide the app you asked for (performance of a contract). We keep the short-lived sign-in logs for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Service providers

Only these providers handle personal data for us, and only to run the parts described above:

  • Supabase: sign-in accounts and sign-in logs.
  • [email provider]: delivers sign-in emails.
  • Apple and Google: confirm your identity when you choose their sign-in. Google also provides ML Kit.

Some of them may process data outside your country. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in account: until you delete it.
  • Sign-in logs: a few days.
  • Data on your phone: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Your choices and rights

  • Export: Profile → Export my data makes a CSV or JSON file of everything the app holds, which you can save or share.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete all data deletes your sign-in account on our servers, then everything on the phone. This needs a connection. If it can’t reach the server, nothing is deleted and you can try again. You can also ask us by email to delete your account.
  • Sign out: Profile → Sign out removes the session from the phone and keeps your data on it.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Email [contact email] and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security

Connections to our sign-in service are encrypted. Your sign-in session is kept in the phone’s secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). Server keys never ship inside the app.

Children

Life Energy is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If a change affects what leaves your phone, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
